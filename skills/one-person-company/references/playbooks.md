# Functional operating playbooks

Read the domains relevant to the mission or current initiative. Every domain connects to the same outcome charter and resource pool. Use existing specialist skills/tools when available and relevant; these playbooks do not assume any integration is connected.

## Strategy, discovery and positioning

Identify the customer, costly problem, competing alternatives, buying context and decision maker. Gather permission-appropriate customer evidence and distinguish user reports from observed behavior. Form an offer and positioning hypothesis; test with a small, measurable initiative. Prioritize uncertainty that could invalidate the business before polishing output. Revisit segment/offer when activation, sales or retention evidence contradicts assumptions.

## Product engineering and production

Translate discovery/support into tickets with outcome linkage and acceptance criteria. Prioritize customer impact, reliability, expected conversion/retention benefit, effort and dependencies. Ship proportionate increments with relevant tests, telemetry and rollback. Verify end-to-end value delivery rather than counting merged code. For non-software production, establish equivalent input quality, capacity, delivery acceptance and rework controls. Monitor cost and bottlenecks before increasing demand. Invoke product/repository expertise only when inspecting or changing that product.

## Opportunity creation, marketing and creative assets

Proactively seek audience access, partnerships, distribution, speaking opportunities, referrals, search demand, community needs and useful content gaps. Build a ranked opportunity backlog with fit, evidence, effort, reach quality, permissions and expected business value. Conference/LinkedIn anecdotes motivate exploration; they do not prove a channel's effectiveness.

Build the message around a specific customer's desired transformation. Trace the mechanism to its immediate result, then ask why that result matters in their life. For example, acne treatment → clearer skin → confidence to ask someone out; an AI team completing work → fewer unfinished tasks → an evening free of work hanging over you. These illustrate the reasoning, not promises to reuse for every audience. Establish the actual segment's payoff from evidence; label assumptions as hypotheses.

Lead with a concrete, credible payoff. Follow with the mechanism, available proof and a clear next step. Review headline and body together: can the intended customer picture what changes for them, understand why this offer can deliver it, and know what to do next? Replace category labels, feature inventories and abstract phrases such as “real momentum” when they obscure that change. Benefits must remain specific and supported; do not manufacture certainty, testimonials, scarcity, queue positions or guaranteed outcomes to improve response. Evaluate variants through qualified signups, completed first value and retention, including support burden and acquisition cost.

Create a campaign brief with customer problem, offer, message, channel, desired next step and attribution plan. Produce blogs, static images, videos or other content with suitable available skills. Verify factual claims, brand/customer fit, asset rights and destination experience. Adapt assets to channel context; reuse proven ideas thoughtfully. Measure qualified demand through activation and retention, not impressions alone. Publishing authority is scoped to the operation and the actual grant; outreach and ad spending require their own grants.

## Outreach and sales

Qualify audience and likely need using reliable evidence; prepare useful, accurate messages and a coherent offer. Specify identity, recipients/audience, exact messaging/variants, follow-up rules, limits and stopping conditions for review when approval is required. Scheduling or staging an outgoing send also requires authority. Use authorized channels and applicable consent/platform constraints. Track replies, qualified opportunities, objections, commitments, conversion and downstream activation; stop or revise poor-fit messaging. Do not promise unverified capabilities, pricing or delivery commitments. Coordinate account ownership and existing customer contacts before sending.

## Onboarding, customer success and support

Define the customer's first value milestone and detect friction with observed journey/cohort evidence. Resolve support cases proportionally; protect private customer data. Route urgent incidents to recovery. Record recurring issues, demand and objections as product evidence; create tickets with impact, acceptance and ownership. Follow the loop: support → prioritized ticket → verified fix/release → customer follow-up when authorized → observed experience/retention impact. Close the ticket on acceptance evidence; keep lagging outcome measurement open when needed. Own adoption, renewal/retention, churn reasons and recovery of unhappy customers within authority.

## Finance and resource allocation

Maintain revenue, refunds, costs, commitments, cash/runway and margin assumptions where data exists. Distinguish cash, recognized revenue and recurring-revenue estimates rather than conflating them. Track acquisition cost by channel with cost inclusions and attribution limits; compare lifetime-value estimates only with defensible retention assumptions. Inventory person/agent time and tool costs. An unknown budget is not unlimited or zero. If spending authority is absent, request an envelope and continue non-spending preparation. If the user explicitly grants budget discretion, record that grant and select a defensible bounded initiative from available financial evidence; do not re-request permission already granted. Missing cash/capacity evidence may still block a responsible commitment: identify that missing fact rather than calling it a missing approval. Account or card access grants no spending authority. Reallocate resources based on incremental customer/outcome value, evidence and constraints; expose tradeoffs and opportunity cost. Pricing changes and financial commitments need the applicable grant.

## Project management and coordination

Keep one prioritized outcome-linked backlog across functions, with explicit owners and manageable work in progress. Agree dependencies and handoff acceptance with other operators. Surface shared-resource conflicts and inconsistent promises to the accountable decision maker. Allocate capacity for reliability/support alongside growth; do not let campaign volume overwhelm delivery. Review initiatives at their evaluation triggers and decide stop/iterate/scale. Propose an operating cadence suited to customer response times, evidence arrival and resource needs; do not impose an arbitrary cadence or create schedules without authority.
