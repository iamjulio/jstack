# Campaign production and acceptance

## Evidence brief

State the audience and its observed problem, offer, next step, primary business outcome and working hypothesis. Use actual customer research, support/sales evidence, product behavior or user-provided facts. Public research can inform a hypothesis; it does not prove demand for this offer.

For each material claim, retain the claim, source, date, permitted wording and limitations. Offer facts such as price, eligibility, deadline and refund terms come from the current approved offer source. A transcript or earlier ad is not proof those facts remain current.

## Versioned artifacts

Keep a stable campaign ID across brief, creative, landing page, tracking plan and canonical ticket. Record revisions rather than overwriting the evidence that was reviewed or activated. Link the exact final asset and preserve any license/consent evidence needed for the actual use.

Before production, define acceptance that a reviewer can verify: audience fit, accurate offer, supported claims, coherent next step, working destination, readable media/accessibility, event capture and applicable channel requirements. Avoid turning taste into an unexplained numeric grade.

Use a small rubric with observable criteria and examples relevant to the channel. Record pass, repair, or unresolved for each material criterion. An aggregate creative score cannot waive a false claim, missing grant or broken destination. Keep automated predictions separate from observed performance.

## Independent review

Select reviewers for actual risk: claims and brand, customer data, channel execution, measurement or economics. Provide the brief, substantiation and final revision without the desired verdict. Fix findings and repeat affected review. Approval of one version is not evidence for changed copy, destination or audience.

For low-impact drafts, direct self-check may be sufficient. Do not add an approval ceremony beyond the user's task and actual policy. When activation authority is missing, finish the concrete assets, preview, settings and experiment design before requesting the final grant.

## Activation and operational QA

Verify destination and tracking in a safe test mode before using real traffic. Record test event ID, event time, parameters, deduplication and expected business record where available. Use permitted synthetic data; do not replay effectful purchases or sends as a test.

Read back actual remote settings after activation, including active/paused state, spend/volume, start/end time, currency/timezone and destination. Capture a live preview when supported. If the campaign is scheduled or paused, report that state precisely.

Monitor only within the authorized run. An instruction to prepare a campaign does not create a recurring monitor or autonomous spend policy. If a longer observation window is needed, persist the next review time and owner; schedule a follow-up only if the user authorized scheduling.

## Decision receipt

Persist the campaign and experiment IDs, creative revision, actual channel settings, actor/grant, activation receipts, measurement source/window, costs, result and uncertainty. Record keep/change/stop/inconclusive and one next decision. Separate creative lessons from targeting, offer, seasonality and instrumentation effects. Never present a low-cost click as a profitable customer without the downstream evidence.
