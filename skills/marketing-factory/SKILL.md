# Marketing factory

Connect marketing work to an observable customer and business outcome. Use the existing queue, asset repository, measurement systems and publication tools. This package supplies procedures and templates; it does not install a campaign runner, send messages or connect an analytics account.

## Bind the organization

Discover the selected organization, accountable owner, audience, offer, canonical queue, versioned asset location, channel identity and business record. Read [project binding](references/project-binding.md) for a first run or missing integration. Use [the profile template](assets/project-profile.json) only when the project has no equivalent. Credentials and customer-level data belong in the project's approved private systems.

Carry forward the user's actual grants, including channel, account, audience, spend/volume caps and expiry. A connection or approved strategy does not authorize an unspecific send, spend increase or audience expansion. Prepare useful drafts and experiments within scope while resolving a missing activation choice. Do not ask again for a grant the session already supplies.

## Choose the mode

- **Operator:** research, position, create, run or evaluate a campaign. Follow [the campaign loop](references/campaign-loop.md) and use [the experiment template](assets/experiment.json).
- **Engineer:** change measurement contracts, adapters, creative graders, routing, review gates or the experiment harness. Follow [factory engineering](references/factory-engineering.md). Track this work separately. Operators cannot loosen their own grading, attribution or spend limits to make a campaign win.

## The campaign loop

1. **Intake:** reconcile existing initiatives and define one audience, customer problem, offer, owner and primary outcome. Persist the work in the canonical queue. Name what must be learned before execution.
2. **Ground:** gather primary audience evidence, offer facts and substantiation for material claims. Separate quoted evidence, observed behavior and hypotheses. Record source and freshness; do not fabricate customers, testimonials, scarcity or results.
3. **Produce:** version the brief, claims ledger, creative, landing destination and tracking plan in the project's asset repository. Match each message to its actual audience, offer and next step. A repo may be Git or a system with equivalent stable revision/history.
4. **Evaluate:** check factual claims, channel eligibility, brand fit, audience relevance, accessibility and destination/tracking behavior against the frozen brief. Use independent review for material claims, spend or sensitive data where the project or risk calls for it. Review the actual creative revision.
5. **Experiment:** record hypothesis, variant/control, allocation, primary metric, sample/window, economic guardrails and stop/decision rule before activation. Choose a design proportionate to traffic and risk; observational evidence remains labeled observational.
6. **Activate:** use the selected channel and current capability schema, read back settings and capture a receipt. Public publication, outreach and paid spend require the applicable grant. A saved draft or paused ad is not a live campaign.
7. **Measure and loop:** validate data, wait for the agreed window/lag, then compare results and uncertainty. Persist keep/change/stop/inconclusive with evidence and a next owner. Close delivery separately from the experiment's eventual outcome.

Read [measurement and experiments](references/measurement.md) before planning or interpreting a test. Do not silently replace business outcomes with impressions, clicks or platform-reported conversions when the primary result is unavailable.

## Recovery and handoffs

Use stable campaign/experiment IDs and remote receipts. If a publish/send/create call times out, inspect remote state before retrying. Prefer provider-enforced idempotency; when unavailable, reconcile by returned object, dedupe key and run window. Stop dependent effects if duplicates cannot be excluded. An unknown send outcome is not permission to resend.

Respect the configured attempt, time, spend and audience limits. Without a retry policy, allow one diagnosed repair and report the remaining blocker. Do not automatically extend a test or increase spend to seek significance. Halt dependent activation for invalid tracking, harmful guardrail movement, expired authority or unresolved material claims; retain useful drafts and analysis.

For a software or analytics dependency, record parent/child ticket, acceptance, asset revision, permitted data, both owners and the returned verification. The receiving team needs its own release authority. Avoid cyclic requests and duplicate budget reservations. Do not treat a handoff record as authorization to message people or other chats.

Finish with artifact and campaign links, current draft/live state, verification, spend or volume against limits, measurement status, result strength and next decision. Identify untested assumptions and incomplete observation windows plainly.
